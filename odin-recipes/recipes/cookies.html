COOKIES RECIPE

Cookies

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of butter
  • 2 cups of White sugar or brown
  • 3 cups All-purpose flour
  • Little bit of salt, 1/2 teaspoon
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 teaspoon Baking soda
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • Chocolate chips (optional)
  • Nuts (optional)

Steps

  1. Mix the Ingredients in a bowl and preheat the oven to 350 degrees
  2. After mixing, drop rounded spoonfuls of cookie dough onto ungreased baking sheets
  3. Bake in the oven until lightly brown, about 10 minutes
  4. leave on baking sheet to cool
  5. Serve or store in airtight container
