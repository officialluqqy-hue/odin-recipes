CHAI RECIPE

CHAI

Chai is a spiced and sweetened black tea mixed with milk.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of white sugar
  • 1 cup of instant tea
  • 2 teaspoons of grounded ginger
  • 1 teaspoon of grounded cloves
  • 1 cup of dry milk powder

Steps

  1. Gather the Ingredients.
  2. Combine the Ingredients in a bowl.
  3. Blend the Ingredients in a food processor or blender until a fine powder.
  4. Stir 2 tablespoons of the blended mix into a mug of hot water for 1 serving.
