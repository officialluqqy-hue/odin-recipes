CHAPATI RECIPE

CHAPATI

Chapati is a flour made dish.

This dish goes into the category of bread. And it is famous in East Africa and India.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup whole flour
  • 1 cup all purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon of salt
  • 3/4 cup of water or as needed
  • 2 tablespoons of cooking oil

Steps

  1. Gather all Ingredients
  2. Mix whole wheat flour, all-purpose flour, and salt in a large bowl. Mix until soft and add more water, if needed.
  3. Knead dough on a lightly floured surface until smooth.
  4. Divide the dough into 10 equal portions. And let rest for a few minutes.
  5. Now use a rolling pin to roll the dough balls out on a lightly floured surface until very thin.
  6. Heat a lightly grealed skillet and when it starts to smoke, place a chapati in it. Cook until brown spots appear on the bottom, about 30 seconds, then flip and cook the side which was up.
  7. Repeat to cook the the remaining chapatis.
